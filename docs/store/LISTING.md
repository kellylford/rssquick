## Product name

RSS Quick

## Short description

An RSS reader built for screen reader and braille display users.

## Description

RSS Quick is a fast, simple RSS reader designed from the start for people who use a screen reader or a braille display.

There are two panels: your feeds, organised in folders, and the headlines of whatever you open. Press Enter on a feed to read its headlines, or on a folder to read every feed in it at once, newest first. Press Enter on a headline and the article opens in your web browser, where your screen reader already knows its way around.

Everything works from the keyboard, and everything says what it did. Focus goes where you expect and stays where you left it. Headlines are cleaned of the invisible characters and stray spacing that show up as blank cells on a braille display. The status bar tells you where you are, what a load found, and which feeds failed, without interrupting you to do it.

RSS Quick starts with a list of feeds covering world news, business, technology, science, the arts, sports and accessibility, and you can make it your own: subscribe to a site by its address, remove feeds, import an OPML file from another reader, and export your list to take elsewhere.

RSS Quick collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

## Product features

(Up to 20, each 200 characters or fewer.)

- Two panels, feeds and headlines, each a single Tab stop
- Read one feed, or every feed in a folder at once, newest first
- Search the headlines of every feed with /
- Subscribe to a site by its web address or its feed address
- Import and export your feed list as OPML
- Articles open in your own web browser
- Headlines cleaned of invisible characters for braille displays
- Status bar reports position, load results and failed feeds
- Full keyboard control, with shortcuts listed under Help
- Follows Windows high contrast themes and text size
- No accounts, no advertising, no tracking

The answers are Kelly's to give; these are what the app does.

- Category: an app that is not a game. Closest fit is news or reference.
- Violence, sexual content, language, controlled substances, gambling: none in the app itself.

- **Shares the user's location:** no.
- **Allows purchases:** no.
- **Unrestricted internet access** (a browser, or content from the web the developer does not
